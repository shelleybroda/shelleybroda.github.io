Learning across
legal systems.

Shelley Broda · London · August 2025

An excerpt from my fellowship reflection on the questions I wanted to explore during visits to London law firms.

Original reflection excerpt

What? My goal

My goal is to learn more about how lawyers in London deal with legal problems, case strategy, and relationships with clients, especially when the case involves foreign, cross-border, or global conflicts. I want to see how the range of differences in culture, procedure, and territory affect the way law is practiced here, in London.

So what? Why it matters

This goal matters to me since I'm considering a career that may involve foreign business or international conflicts. Understanding how lawyers handle these conflicts will teach me valuable lessons that I cannot just learn from reading in books or understanding theories alone.

What now? My approach

This will be done by actively participating in meetings during company visits, by asking lawyers about specific cases or client scenarios, and taking careful notes on how these things are different from U.S. practice, but also how they are similar and how they can be applied. I'll also compare what I see with what I already believe, so that I can leave with a reasonable and balanced viewpoint.
